His Story

Lionel Messi was born on 24 June 1987 in Rosario, Argentina. He started playing football at a young age and showed exceptional talent. At the age of 13, he moved to Spain and joined FC Barcelona's youth academy, La Masia.

Messi became one of football's greatest players through his incredible dribbling, vision, passing, creativity and goal scoring. His journey took him from Barcelona to Paris Saint-Germain and eventually to Inter Miami.

His greatest international achievement came on 18 December 2022, when he captained Argentina to victory in the FIFA World Cup final against France.

03 | CLUB CAREER

FC BARCELONA

2004 - 2021

Barcelona was the club where Messi became a global superstar. He developed into an extraordinary goalscorer and playmaker, forming legendary partnerships with players such as Xavi, Andres Iniesta, Luis Suarez and Neymar.

  • 10 La Liga titles
  • 4 UEFA Champions League titles
  • 7 Copa del Rey titles
  • 3 FIFA Club World Cup titles
  • 8 Spanish Super Cups
  • 3 UEFA Super Cups

Legacy: Barcelona's all-time leading goalscorer.

PARIS SAINT-GERMAIN

2021 - 2023

Messi joined Paris Saint-Germain after leaving Barcelona. He played alongside Neymar and Kylian Mbappe and helped PSG win domestic titles in France.

  • 2 Ligue 1 titles
  • 1 French Champions Trophy

INTER MIAMI

2023 - Present

In July 2023, Messi joined Inter Miami. His arrival brought worldwide attention to the club and Major League Soccer. He helped Miami win its first Leagues Cup in 2023, the Supporters' Shield in 2024 and the MLS Cup in 2025.

  • Leagues Cup: 2023
  • Supporters' Shield: 2024
  • MLS Cup: 2025
